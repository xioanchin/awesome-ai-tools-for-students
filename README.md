# Best Free AI Tools for Students in 2026

A curated list of the best AI tools for students, organized by use case. Every tool links to a full review with pricing details and community ratings. Free and freemium tools are prioritized because students should not have to pay to study smarter.

---

## Research and Literature Review

**[NotebookLM](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/notebooklm)** — Free  
Google's AI notebook for source-grounded Q&A and summaries. Upload your lecture notes, textbooks, or research papers and ask questions directly. One of the most useful free AI tools available for students right now.

**[Elicit](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/elicit)** — Freemium  
AI research assistant for finding and synthesizing academic papers. Saves hours of manual literature review by extracting key findings across dozens of papers at once.

**[Consensus](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/consensus)** — Freemium  
AI-powered academic search engine that pulls evidence-backed answers directly from scientific literature. Useful for finding sources that actually support your argument.

**[Scite.ai](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/scite-ai)** — Freemium  
Shows how research papers are cited across other papers, whether supporting or contradicting the original claim. Essential for evaluating source quality in academic writing.

**[Perplexity AI](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/perplexity-ai)** — Freemium  
AI answer engine with real-time search and inline citations. Better than a search engine for research questions because it synthesizes answers instead of just returning links.

## Writing and Editing

**[Grammarly](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/grammarly)** — Freemium  
AI writing assistant for grammar, tone, clarity, and style. Works across browsers and apps so it catches errors wherever you write, not just in one editor.

**[QuillBot](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/quillbot)** — Freemium  
AI paraphrasing, summarizing, grammar checking, and citation generator in one tool. The summarizer alone saves hours when reading dense academic texts.

**[Wordtune](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/wordtune)** — Freemium  
Rewrites sentences for fluency, clarity, and tone. Useful for students writing in a second language or anyone who wants to improve sentence quality without changing meaning.

**[Rytr](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/rytr)** — Freemium  
AI writing assistant for emails, short essays, outlines, and study content. One of the most affordable options for students who need writing help on a budget.

## Study and Note Taking

**[Otter.ai](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/otter-ai)** — Freemium  
Records and transcribes lectures in real time with AI-generated summaries. Never miss a key point from a fast-talking professor again.

**[Notion AI](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/notion-ai)** — Freemium  
AI-powered workspace for notes, study plans, and knowledge organization. Students who already use Notion get a significant upgrade with the AI layer built in.

**[Julius AI](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/julius-ai)** — Freemium  
AI data analysis tool for spreadsheets, statistics, and research data. Useful for students in economics, science, or any course that involves datasets and charts.

## AI Assistants and Chat

**[ChatGPT](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/chatgpt)** — Freemium  
The most widely used AI assistant for writing, research, coding, and studying. The free tier is genuinely useful for most student tasks.

**[Claude](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/claude)** — Freemium  
Anthropic's AI assistant with strong reasoning and writing capabilities. Particularly good at analyzing long documents and giving detailed, structured explanations.

**[Google Gemini](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/google-gemini)** — Freemium  
Google's AI assistant with deep integration into Google Docs, Gmail, and Search. Students already in the Google ecosystem benefit most from this.

**[Microsoft Copilot](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/microsoft-copilot)** — Freemium  
Microsoft's AI assistant built into Word, Excel, and other Office apps. Free for students at many universities through Microsoft 365 Education.

**[Perplexity AI](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/perplexity-ai)** — Freemium  
Better than Google for research questions. Answers with sources so you can verify and cite directly.

## Coding and Technical Subjects

**[Replit AI](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/replit-ai)** — Freemium  
Browser-based coding environment with built-in AI assistance. No installation needed, which makes it ideal for students learning to code on any device.

**[Hugging Face](https://usefuturestack.com/tools/hugging-face)** — Freemium  
Open platform for AI models, datasets, and machine learning tools. Essential for computer science and data science students exploring ML.

**[Derivative Calculator AI](https://derivativecalculatorai.com/)** — Free  
Step-by-step calculus solver for derivatives, integrals and limits, with AI explanations, answer checking and practice problems. No sign-up needed.
